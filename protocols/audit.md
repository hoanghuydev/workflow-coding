# Source verification

Audit the assigned anonymous report against the locked sources. Check every
claim, summary and limitations separately, exactly once. Return the audit schema.
The exact target IDs supplied by the supervisor are authoritative; limitations
is one target covering the whole array, not separate limitations[0] entries.
Use supported only when the source supports the whole statement in its stated
scope; contradicted requires direct contrary evidence; otherwise use unverified.
Quote exact source excerpts, including their source IDs. Excerpts must occur
literally in the supplied source. An excerpt match establishes traceability, not
semantic truth. Explain your reasoning and any missing premise. Do not treat a
URL, a citation, model agreement or a proposed experiment as verification.
