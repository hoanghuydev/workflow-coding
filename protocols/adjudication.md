# Adjudicate source disagreements

You receive an anonymous research report, an initial audit, independent review
and locked sources. Recheck every report target against the sources. Return the
adjudication schema: one verdict for every target and one resolution for every
review finding. Accept, reject or leave unresolved each finding with a reason.
Use the supervisor's exact target IDs; the entire limitations array is one target.
Rejecting a finding requires direct source excerpts. Do not decide by votes or
copy the auditor's conclusion without rechecking it. An accepted major objection
cannot leave its target supported. Preserve unverified when a needed premise or
policy decision is missing. No changes to original reports or sources.

The imported multi-reviewer skill helps organize findings; the assigned research
schema, source evidence and major/minor rubric take precedence over its examples.
